Plans are billed per farm, not per person, and every plan includes unlimited crew members. Prices are shown before tax; sales tax is added at checkout where it applies. A free trial lasts thirty days and can be cancelled at any time from the account page, after which the notebook stays readable but cannot be edited. Weather data is provided by public forecast services and may be late or wrong; always check local conditions before acting on a frost or heat warning shown in the planner.

Yield estimates are based on the records you enter and on regional averages for each crop, and are not a promise of any harvest. Seed counts assume the germination rates printed on the packet. Exports are available as spreadsheets at any time, and your data is deleted within thirty days of closing an account unless you ask us to keep it longer for your records.
