Slow light over the dunes

Notes from a season of walking the same coastline every morning, written down before breakfast.

Every entry starts with the tide table and the colour of the sky at seven. Some mornings the fog stays until noon; some mornings there is nothing to write except the wind direction and how far the water came up the beach overnight.
